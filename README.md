# ATX-Bench-Power-Supply

A DIY Bench ATX Power Supply enclosure and front panel.

## Features

- Custom Voltage & Amperage adjustment
- 4x Default voltage outputs (3.3V, 5V, 2x 12V)
- 4x Banana Plugs for GND
- 4x Banana Plugs for Volts
- 1x Custom voltage output (1.25V to 12V)
- 1x P4 Barrel Jack connector (12V)

## Notes

- I used a Corsair ATX PSU with dimensions of 150 x 130 x 85mm. Since other power supplies may vary in size—as was the case with Nozzle1's project—this model might not fit your specific PSU.
- I had to split the model into 2 parts because my printer maxes out at 250mm in height, but if you have a larger printer, feel free to print it all at once!

## Materials

- 1x P4 Barrel Jack Socket (Female)
- 2x 10K Linear Potentiometers

- 1x Digital Voltmeter & Ammeter Display (0-100V / 10A)
- 1x KCD1-101 Rocker Switch (2-Pin, Red)
- 1x ATX Power Supply
- 4x M3 x 5.7 x 4.2mm Heat-Set Inserts
- 4x M3 Screws
